# Travel browse

Web, iOS, and Android use the same Search screen. The website publish on main serves this Search. An installed app keeps the previous Search until the next store build.

A paid PlateTaker can look at plates in another city. Ordering stays on the current rule: the saved location has to be within 20 miles of that kitchen, and the pickup time has to be on a day and hour that plate is offered.

## PlateTaker

Signed out, Search is not on screen.

Signed in, free, Search says another city is for Premium. The menu stays inside 20 miles. Add to Plate stays on a plate inside that circle.

Signed in, Premium, Search has a city field. New York shows plates from New York-Newark-Jersey City, including a person whose saved place is China or any other country. The card says look only when that kitchen is more than 20 miles away. Opening the plate shows the food photos, the food videos, and the cook’s profile photo. Add to Plate is not on that plate. It returns when the saved location is inside 20 miles, and checkout still requires a pickup time on a day and hour the plate is offered.

## PlateMaker

Signed out, Search is not on screen.

Signed in, Search has no city field. A cook plan does not open another city. The menu stays inside 20 miles. A cook does not order their own plate.
