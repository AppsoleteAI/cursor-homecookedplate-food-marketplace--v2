# Food photos

Web, iOS, and Android use `FoodImage`. A plate photo, a cart photo, an order photo, and a listed food photo all use that crop. A profile photo, a license, and a permit stay as they are.

The frame covers the photo. A wide photo stays centered from side to side. A tall photo shifts so the dish, which usually sits a little below the middle, lands in the middle of the frame. The home banner is that short frame, so the cake sits in the center instead of along the bottom edge.

## Both outcomes

Signed out, Home is not on screen.

Signed in, a PlateMaker and a PlateTaker see the same crop on Home, Search, and a plate. Clips stays a video. A PlateTaker also sees it on Cart and Orders. A PlateMaker also sees it on the kitchen order row, on a plate being created, and on the photo attached to a listed dish, farm good, truck item, prep item, or catering package. A photo of a kitchen, a truck, a restaurant, or a stand stays centered on the whole photo.

A push to main publishes this on the website. The iOS and Android store builds stay on the previous copy until a new build.
