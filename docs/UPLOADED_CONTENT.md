# Uploaded content

Web, iOS, and Android use the same Legal & Safety screen. The website publish includes this section. An app already on a phone keeps the previous agreement until the next store build.

Both a PlateMaker and a PlateTaker check this section before sign-in. A saved acknowledgment that does not include Uploaded content is incomplete, so that person checks every section again and enters today's date.

## PlateMaker

Signed out: the cook opens Legal & Safety, checks Uploaded content, and enters today's date before sign-in.

Signed in: a cook whose saved acknowledgment already includes this section sees it recorded. A cook whose saved acknowledgment does not include it confirms the page again before the account stores the new agreement.

The cook's meal names, ingredient and allergen text, photos, and video files must be original, or the cook must already have permission to upload them. The cook keeps ownership. HomeCookedPlate and its Appsolete affiliates receive a worldwide license to host, store, copy, display, and use that upload to run and promote the marketplace. That license remains after the cook removes a listing or closes the account, including copies kept for a backup, a safety review, or the record of an order.

A YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, or Reddit link on a listing or clip is not an upload of that video. The cook pastes a link only when the cook has the right to share it. HomeCookedPlate does not own the linked video.

## PlateTaker

Signed out: the buyer opens Legal & Safety, checks Uploaded content, and enters today's date before sign-in.

Signed in: a buyer whose saved acknowledgment already includes this section sees it recorded. A buyer whose saved acknowledgment does not include it confirms the page again before the account stores the new agreement.

A buyer's clip text, photos, and video files follow the same original-content rule and the same license. A YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, or Reddit link is not an upload of that video.

## Copy on the page

What you upload must be original. Meal names, ingredient and allergen text, photos, and video files must be yours, or you must already have permission to upload them. A PlateMaker's listing and a PlateTaker's clip follow this same rule.

A YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, or Reddit link is not an upload of that video. Paste a link only when you have the right to share it. HomeCookedPlate does not own the linked video.

You keep ownership of what you upload. You give HomeCookedPlate and its Appsolete affiliates a worldwide license to host, store, copy, display, and use that uploaded content to run and promote the marketplace. HomeCookedPlate retains that license after you remove a listing or close the account, including copies kept for a backup, a safety review, or the record of an order.

The final acknowledgment on that page names the uploaded-content terms.
