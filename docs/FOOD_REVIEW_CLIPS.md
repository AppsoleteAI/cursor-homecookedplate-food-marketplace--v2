# FoodReviewClips

Web, iOS, and Android use the same FoodReviewClips screen. The website publish includes this screen. An app already installed on a phone keeps the previous clips screen until the next store build.

The post card stays under the green header. The clips scroll underneath it. Up and down chevrons in the lower right jump to the top and the bottom of that list. Those chevrons use the FoodReviewClips green, `#16A34A` to `#86EFAC`, at 33 pixels with a 17-pixel icon.

The timeline shows two newest clips, then the popular clip with the highest smile, heart, and star total that is not already on screen, then repeats. A PlateMaker and a PlateTaker see that same order. Search and Bookmarks narrow the list before that order is applied. Hide removes a clip on this device.

A verified food influencer has ✅ immediately after `@screenname`. `@DjYoyoFromMiami` is that verified post, and the clip is the Instagram reel `DMWBHRruXvj`. The fourth seed is `@belieber_qal` on Snapchat. On screen that Snapchat clip is second, because it is the second-newest post. Orlando is the first popular clip.

The three-dot chip stays in the upper right of the clip. It opens the source page, reports the clip on this device, or hides the clip. Playback stays in the frame. A post that does not fill the 9:16 frame is centered, with the empty black split above and below.

After 5 seconds without a scroll, a touch move, or device motion, the three-dot chip, the jump chevrons, and every back arrow fade out. Movement brings them back. Back arrows keep their honey or light color and use the same 33-by-17 size. Web, iOS, and Android share this. This note is local until the site and the store apps are published.

## PlateMaker

Signed in or signed out, the post card is pinned. A free cook sees “Posting is for premium members.” Meal name and the link field stay locked until Premium, lifetime, or a paid subscription. Record and Choose Clip open Membership. Post Link does the same until they can post.

A cook who can post records a clip, chooses a video file, or pastes a link. The link can be YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, X, or a direct video file. That link plays in the clip frame. Bookmarks sits under that card. The bookmark on a clip saves it on this device. Bookmarks opens that list. All clips returns to the timeline.

## PlateTaker

Signed in or signed out, the same post card is pinned, with Search clips above it. A free buyer sees the same premium lock. Premium is what unlocks Record, Choose Clip, and Post Link. Watching a clip does not require Premium.

Bookmarks sits under that card for a buyer too. The saved list stays on this device. The chevrons and the in-frame player are the same as the cook timeline.

YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, and X links play in the frame on the web. On iOS and Android the same embed plays in the clip. A video file plays in the video player. A link posted to the shared kitchen also needs the API worker published.
