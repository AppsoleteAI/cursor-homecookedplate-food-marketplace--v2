Privacy Policy

HomeCookedPlate · Appsolete · Updated 8 October 2026

This policy describes the information HomeCookedPlate collects when you create an account, order food, or sell food. It applies to the iOS app, the Android app, and the website.

Information you give us

  • Account details: email address, username, password, and whether you are a PlateMaker or a PlateTaker.
  • Profile and listing media: a profile picture, meal photos, and food review clips you choose or record. The photo library is used for those listings. Photos are not shared with friends from this app.
  • Microphone audio only when you record a food review clip.
  • Messages you send to another user in the app.
  • Orders, reviews, ingredient and allergen text, and the legal-and-safety acknowledgment you date in the app.

Location

While the app is open, HomeCookedPlate can read your location to check that you are in a supported metro. The app does not collect location in the background.

Payments

Card payments and PlateMaker payouts are processed by Stripe. HomeCookedPlate stores order amounts, payment status, and Stripe customer or connected-account identifiers. Full card numbers and bank account numbers are collected by Stripe, not stored in the app.

A PlateTaker pays the listed price plus a 10% service fee. A PlateMaker payout is the listed price minus a 10% platform fee. Delivery fees, when used, are set by the PlateMaker.

Notifications and diagnostics

  • If you allow notifications, we store an Expo push token so the app can send order, message, and payment alerts. Notifications are sent while you are using the account. The app does not schedule exact alarms or restart work when the phone boots.
  • If crash reporting is enabled for a build, Sentry receives crash diagnostics that can include your account id, email, and username.
  • The app stores a random account-binding token in the device keychain or keystore. It does not use an advertising id or a permanent hardware identifier to identify the device.

How we use information

We use this information to run accounts, show plates in your metro, take orders, pay PlateMakers, moderate uploaded images, send the notifications you turn on, and keep the service secure. We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for cross-app advertising.

Service providers

Supabase hosts the account database and uploaded media. Stripe processes payments and payouts. Expo delivers push notifications. Sentry receives crash reports when that build includes a Sentry project. Cloudflare hosts the website and API. These providers process data for HomeCookedPlate.

How long we keep it

Account, order, and message records stay until you delete the account or we remove the account under the terms. Deleting the account cancels an active membership, removes uploaded media, deletes meals, reviews, and orders tied to the account, anonymizes message content, and deletes the login. A deletion record is kept in an audit log without the deleted profile.

Your choices

In Privacy & Security you can export your data, pause the account, change the password, and delete the account. You can turn notifications off in the app or in the phone settings. You can deny camera, photo, microphone, or location access; the related feature will not work without that permission.

Children

HomeCookedPlate is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.

Contact
