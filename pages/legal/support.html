Support

HomeCookedPlate · Appsolete · Updated 10 October 2026

Write to support@homecookedplate.com. Include the account email and, for an order, the order details and what went wrong. Replies are sent within 48 hours.

Account

Password changes, account pause, data export, and account deletion are in the app under Privacy & Security. Deleting the account cancels an active membership and removes the login.

Orders and payouts

Plate orders are paid by card through Stripe. A PlateMaker payout is released seven days after payment when the sale is still in good standing. Membership on iPhone is billed by Apple. Membership on Android is billed by Google Play. Restore a membership from the Membership screen with the same Apple ID or Google account.
