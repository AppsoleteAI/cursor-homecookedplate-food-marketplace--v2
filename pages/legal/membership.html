HomeCookedPlate · Appsolete · Updated 26 October 2026

HomeCookedPlate is a U.S. food marketplace. PlateMakers cook and list food. PlateTakers order it. Pickup is in a public place during daylight. If a prepaid delivery is agreed upon, we suggest the cook brings someone else along for safety. Alcohol is not sold.

Who it is for

PlateMakers list homemade food in Home Kitchens, FarmGrownBasket, MealPrepGo, CaterEventDeliver, FoodTruckPopup, and SitDownDelicious. A PlateMaker cooks. Membership is how Premium starts.

PlateTakers order for pickup. The app is free. Premium is optional at $7.99 a month or $75.99 a year.

Price

Membership is an optional auto-renewable subscription. The download stays free.

PlateMaker

  • Monthly: $4.99, billed every month.
  • Yearly: $39.99, billed every year.

PlateTaker

  • The app is free.
  • Monthly Premium: $7.99, billed every month.
  • Yearly Premium: $75.99, billed every year.

PlateTaker Premium cuts the service fee on the receipt from 10% to 5% on orders of $20 or more.

On iPhone, Apple bills the subscription. On Android, Google Play bills the subscription.

  • Premium features access
  • Priority support
  • Cancel anytime

Cancel on iPhone under Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions. Cancel on Android in Google Play subscriptions. Restore a membership from the Membership screen with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Plate orders

Plate orders are paid by card through Stripe. The buyer pays the listed price plus a 10% service fee. A Premium PlateTaker pays 5% on an order of $20 or more. The cook's payout is the listed price minus 10%, released seven days after payment when the sale is still in good standing. When a cook sets a delivery fee, the buyer pays that amount plus 10%, and the cook receives the amount they set. The cook enters ingredients and allergens. HomeCookedPlate provides the app and payment tooling. It does not cook the meal.
